Computer Vision Lab (CVLab)

College of Computing and Artificial Intelligence, United Arab Emirates University

Director: Dr. Shengcai Liao · IEEE Fellow, IAPR Fellow

Overview

To push the frontiers of computer vision research by integrating cutting-edge advances in multimodal large language models, generative AI for images and videos, and intelligent visual understanding systems. The lab seeks to develop innovative algorithms, scalable architectures, and real-world applications that enable machines to perceive, reason, and create across diverse modalities.

Through interdisciplinary collaboration, talent cultivation, and partnerships with academia, industry, and society, the lab aims to address pressing challenges in fields such as autonomous systems, digital content creation, healthcare, and scientific discovery. The lab also aims at world-class research and publications and improving UAEU's reputation and impact internationally.

Research

Research Interests

  • Generative AI for Images and Videos
  • Embodied AI for Humanoid/Social Robots
  • Multimodal Large Language Models for Vision
  • Intelligent Visual Understanding Systems
  • AI for Healthcare and Scientific Discovery
  • Scalable Architectures and Real-World Applications
